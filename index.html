About Me

Dharshana V

I am a classically trained artist and have trained in Carnatic music for over fifteen years. I hold a BA in Carnatic Music from PSG College of Arts and Science and an MA in Indian Music from the University of Madras. Alongside classical music, I perform Indian film music as a solo artist and with bands, and I release independent singles on major streaming platforms.

I founded Svanā Academy in 2024 and also teach at Spardha School of Music. My classes build vocal technique, pitch, rhythm and performance skills. Every lesson is planned around the student, with personal guidance at each step, and I prepare students for open mics, competitions and musical events.

  • MA Indian Music, University of Madras (2022–2024)
  • BA Carnatic Music, PSG College of Arts and Science (2019–2022)
  • Founder & Instructor, Svanā Academy · Instructor, Spardha School of Music
  • 50+ stage shows · Vocals for short films and series
